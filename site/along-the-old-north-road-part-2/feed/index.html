http://maps.google.com/maps/ms?ie=UTF&msa=0&msid=113151172367610624957.00048fc743d7f3e8f7726

You could do it in a 4wd.

The “old dirt short cut” starts at the corner of Moorina Road, and Mountain View Road, Moorina. Head up Mountain View Road. It eventually turns into Rieck Road, and becomes very steep and rocky. You should exercise care here. After a couple of km it flattens out into a dirt track and you end up at Ocean View Road. Turn right to head up to Mt Mee.

Hope this helps.
