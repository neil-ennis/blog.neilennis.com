The higher (and older) slab hut is Cook’s hut, owned by two brothers (late 1800s or early 1900s). One of the brothers was speared in the leg after an altercation with local aboriginals and they tried to get him to town to get medical help but they only made it a few kms before he died. There is a cross for him carved in a big gum old tree that’s facing away from the road where he died. I grew up in one of the farms you passed and used to camp in the huts as a kid.
