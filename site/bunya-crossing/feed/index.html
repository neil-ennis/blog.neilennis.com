]]> By: Chris Bailey /bunya-crossing/comment-page-1/#comment-10001 Thu, 29 Aug 2019 01:53:29 +0000 http://blog.neilennis.com/?p=1075#comment-10001 Hi I used to ride horses to Bunya Crossing from Aspley from the mid 60’s until early 70’s until I got a car. Often on a Sunday young people would ride from many suburbs and it was common to see 50 plus horses there. Also it must have been a good short cut as I often saw gravel trucks crossing Bunya on their way to and from the gravel plants at Albany Creek. I am in my mid 60’s now but still go there with old horse riding mates to have a barbeque. Cheers Chris
