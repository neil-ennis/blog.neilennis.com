Hi Peter, here’s one idea:
Start at Dundas Rd, go down Dundas Rd and Whoah Boy Break.
At the bottom, turn right through the “Private Property” sign (they don’t mind mountain bikers) and follow their steep driveway down to Banks Creek Road.
Follow Banks Creek Rd into Fernvale.
Return via England Creek Road to the crossroads at the bottom of Joyners Ridge Rd. It’s steep and rough in one or two spots.
There are two ways up to the crossroads from Fernvale. Take the northernmost (left-hand) option. The southern (right-hand) way up is hard work. You can see what I’m referring to here: https://goo.gl/LmlMPJ
At the crossroads, climb back up via Joyners Ridge Road. It’s the easiest of the 3 alternatives. If you don’t mind the extra distance, return via England Ck and Lawton Road – it’s scenic, but it’s a long climb.
Then follow Mt Glorious Rd back to Dundas Rd to get your car.
Avoid Goodes Road. It’s awesome either way on an MTB, but dangerous on CX.
If you need GPX let me know.
Neil
