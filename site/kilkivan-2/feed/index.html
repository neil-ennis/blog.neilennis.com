]]> By: Will Milford /kilkivan-2/comment-page-1/#comment-12102 Sun, 16 May 2021 05:49:10 +0000 https://blog.neilennis.com/?p=15097#comment-12102 Did Widgee Mountain Road on Friday. Spectacular road. Reminiscent of the Goat Track or The Link Rd off Maclean Rd S. Steep but ridable and in good condition. I did it with a loaded gravel bike and made it the whole way up with a couple of stops (for pictures!).

Once over the top the road steadily deteriorates and there is still some pitchy sections. The top of Black Snake Rd is rough but doable to the lakes and then there is an amazing descent on a sealed road (?!) before rolling into Kilkivan.
