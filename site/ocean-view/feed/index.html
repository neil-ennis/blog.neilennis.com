]]> By: Brian /ocean-view/comment-page-1/#comment-6829 Sat, 27 Dec 2014 03:50:33 +0000 http://blog.neilennis.com/?p=2695#comment-6829 Just browsing, saw your site. We live at 21 Dean Drive, Ocean View. You would’ve dropped down from Dean Drive and gone through the Reserve (to Thornhill Chase) on a firebreak which is cleared regularly by the council. If you had turned right about 200m from the top of this break, there is a break that follows the contour along the bottom of 13-31 Dean Drive properties. This eventually comes out near the end of Dean Drive’s cul-de-sac. From there you can drop down the waterfall adjacent to this area, or skip through to Zillman Rd. Hope that helps.
Cheers!
