Craig, title searches show 101 / SP303645 as “Unallocated State Land” owned by “The State of Queensland”.

The Qld Govt website says: unallocated state land refers to land above and below HWM that is not freehold land or land contracted to be granted in fee simple by the state; is not a road or reserve; and is not subject to a lease, licence or permit issued by the state.”

Therefore:
1. There is no lease on the land.
2. It’s owned by the State Government
3. It’s not a national park or forest reserve

I’m not a lawyer, but I don’t think the state government would get upset about someone crossing unallocated state land.
