Sometimes land owners erect misleading signs to discourage activity near their land.
I think that if you stay within the white areas you should be ok.
Also, I would not recommend trying to cross this actual bridge. Go down to the creek, and cross it.
The same applies for the smaller railway bridges near Woolooga town.
