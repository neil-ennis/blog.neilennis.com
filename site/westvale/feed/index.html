Hi Peter
When we did this ride 9 years ago, there were no angry “Private Property” signs. At the time I thought I was following road reserves for most of the way except for a hundred metres or so.
Obviously I was wrong.
I couple of friends of mine have had run-ins with the land owner, so my advice is (unless he gives the ok) don’t try to pass through the property.
As an alternative, you could follow our course in reverse west from Neurum Creek and just follow Westvale Road to the end. It’s a nice ride.
Regards
Neil
